Two readers disagree about who speaks one quote in a story. Decide who is right.

You get the excerpt and clues computed from the text:
- the words right before and right after the quote. A speech tag there ("said Jane", "He asked", "the guy offered") names the speaker; resolve he/she to the person the text was just talking about. An action sentence about someone else ("Anesh nodded at him") is NOT a speech tag.
- other quotes in the same paragraph with their speaker: without a tag of its own, a quote usually continues the speaker of its paragraph.
- the speaker of the previous paragraph: in a two-person exchange without tags, speakers alternate.

Steps: tag (copy it, or "none"), reason, answer (one of the options).
